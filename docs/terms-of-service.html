3. Local-First Architecture & User Security Responsibility

OdinEye is engineered on an uncompromising Local-First, Zero-Knowledge Privacy Model. We do not host remote user accounts or centralized database servers.

🛡️ ON-DEVICE ENCRYPTED VAULT Your health records, biometric sync tokens, medication reminders, and mood check-ins are encrypted via AES-256-CBC and stored strictly inside your phone’s sandboxed private storage (Linux Mode 0700).

Because encryption keys and personal health records reside strictly on your physical Android hardware:

  • You are solely responsible for maintaining physical device security, biometric locks, screen PINs, and backup hygiene.
  • If you uninstall the Application, clear app data via Android system settings, or tap "Wipe Vault & Delete Keys", your cryptographic keys and historical records are permanently destroyed. We cannot recover lost local data.
