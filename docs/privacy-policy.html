Privacy Policy & Health Data Governance

OdinEye Privacy Policy

Application: OdinEye (com.odineye.health) • Effective Date: September 13, 2026 • Contact: N/A

1. Core Architecture: Local-First & Zero-Knowledge

OdinEye is engineered on a strict local-first computing architecture. We do not operate, host, or maintain centralized cloud servers, tracking pixels, or remote databases that collect, transmit, or process your personal health records.

Zero Plaintext & Zero Monetization Guarantee

Your health and biometric data remains exclusively on your Android device in hardware-isolated encrypted storage. We never sell, lease, broker, trade, or monetize your health information under any circumstances.

2. Android Health Connect Data (Read-Only)

With your explicit, granular permission, OdinEye accesses data from Android Health Connect strictly in read-only mode to synthesize your daily recovery score, activity pillars, and sleep telemetry:

Data Category Android Health Permission Purpose in Application
Sleep android.permission.health.READ_SLEEP Sleep duration, cycles, and restorative index
Heart Rate & RHR READ_HEART_RATE, READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE Cardiovascular strain & recovery baselines
HRV READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITY Autonomic nervous system readiness & HRV trends
Exercise android.permission.health.READ_EXERCISE Workout duration and muscle fatigue timers
Calories READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED, READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED Daily energy expenditure equilibrium
Steps & Distance READ_STEPS, READ_DISTANCE Daily step targets & walking volume
Vitals READ_SKIN_TEMPERATURE, READ_OXYGEN_SATURATION, READ_RESPIRATORY_RATE Nocturnal recovery & respiratory equilibrium

3. Google Play Health Connect Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

OdinEye strictly complies with the Google Play Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use Requirements:

  • Specific Permitted Use: Health Connect data is accessed solely to provide user-facing wellness, fitness, and recovery features visible directly inside the OdinEye user interface.
  • No Commercial Sale or Transfer: Health Connect data is never sold, rented, leased, traded, or transferred to any third party, data broker, advertising exchange, or data aggregator.
  • No Advertising or Marketing: Health Connect data is never used or disclosed for advertising, retargeting, promotional communications, profiling, or interest-based marketing.
  • No Creditworthiness or Lending Evaluation: Health Connect data is never used to assess, determine, or evaluate creditworthiness, consumer credit eligibility, or lending terms.
  • Prohibition of Human Access: No human is permitted to read user Health Connect data unless explicit user consent is provided for technical support, required by law, or aggregated and de-identified.

4. AI Coach & Telemetry Processing

  • On-Device AI (Android AICore / Gemini Nano): Whenever supported by your hardware, AI inference runs 100% locally inside device NPU memory. Biometrics never leave the device.
  • Offline Rule Engine: A built-in sports science knowledge base operates entirely offline with zero network connectivity.
  • Optional Cloud Gemini API: If you supply your personal Gemini API key, requests are sent directly via encrypted HTTPS to Google AI Studio. You can disable AI at any time in Config.

5. Data Retention & Unilateral User Deletion

Because all data is stored on-device and not on remote servers, you maintain complete unilateral control:

  • In-App Data Deletion: Open OdinEye → Config → Category 04 (Vault Audit) → Tap Wipe Vault & Delete Keys. All tokens, medication records, mindfulness streak logs, and local caches are instantly and permanently erased.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling OdinEye from your Android device immediately destroys all sandboxed local databases and encrypted keys.

6. Legal Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Users in the European Union (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA) possess full rights to access, rectify, port, and delete their data. We do not sell or share personal or sensitive health data.

7. Medical Advice Disclaimer

Informational & Wellness Purposes Only

OdinEye is not a certified medical device and is not evaluated by the FDA or EMA. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a certified physician for medical decisions.
