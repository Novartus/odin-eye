1. Core Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to OdinEye ("we", "our", or "the Application"). Package Identifier: com.odineye.health.

🔒 ZERO CLOUD LEAKAGE GUARANTEE OdinEye does not transmit your physiological metrics, sleep stages, medication schedules, or voice interactions to any cloud database or developer server. All processing executes 100% locally on your smartphone.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate, host, or maintain centralized cloud databases or telemetry backends.
  • Hardware-Backed Encryption: Telemetry caches and medication schedules are encrypted with AES-256-CBC with PBKDF2 key derivation.
  • Zero Data Monetization: We never sell, rent, broker, trade, or monetize your health information under any circumstances.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Handled

2.1 Android Health Connect Permissions (Strictly Read-Only)

With your explicit, granular permission granted via Android Health Connect, OdinEye reads the following biometric and activity data types to generate your daily equilibrium scores and 4-stage sleep hypnogram:

Data Type Android Permission Operational Purpose
Sleep Stages & Duration READ_SLEEP Visualizing Deep (N3), REM, Light, and Awake durations and computing sleep efficiency.
Heart Rate Telemetry READ_HEART_RATE Real-time cardiovascular tracking, cardio zone distribution, and nocturnal resting pulse.
Resting Heart Rate (RHR) READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE Establishing autonomic cardiovascular baselines and nocturnal pulse dip calculation.
Heart Rate Variability (HRV) READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITY Computing parasympathetic autonomic readiness and recovery equilibrium.
Daily Steps & Cadence READ_STEPS Tracking physical activity against custom daily movement goals.
Active Calories Burned READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED Evaluating active cardiovascular exertion and metabolic expenditure.
Skin Temperature READ_SKIN_TEMPERATURE Detecting circadian thermoregulatory dips associated with deep restorative sleep.
Oxygen Saturation (SpO2) READ_OXYGEN_SATURATION Nocturnal respiratory stability and hypoxic indicator evaluation.

Strict Read-Only Access: OdinEye accesses Android Health Connect strictly in read-only mode. The Application never writes, edits, alters, or deletes any records in your Health Connect store.

2.2 Third-Party Fitness Wearable APIs (Client-Side Direct)

If you choose to sync third-party wearables (Ultrahuman Ring AIR, Fitbit Web API, Hevy Strength), OdinEye connects directly from your smartphone via secure HTTPS using credentials you provide. No tokens or biometrics ever transit through any intermediary servers.

2.3 User-Inputted Health Data

Any information you record directly—such as medication names, schedules, dosages, completed breathwork sessions, and self-reported mood ratings—is stored strictly within your phone's sandboxed encrypted storage.

3. Google Play Health Connect Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

OdinEye strictly complies with the Google Play Health Connect Developer Policy and its Limited Use Requirements:

  1. Specific Permitted Use Only: Health Connect data is accessed exclusively to calculate and display user-facing health, sleep, and recovery metrics directly inside the OdinEye application UI.
  2. No Sale or Transfer: We NEVER sell, license, rent, or transfer Health Connect data to any third party, advertising exchange, or data aggregator.
  3. No Advertising or Marketing: Health Connect data is NEVER used or disclosed for personalized advertising, retargeting, promotional campaigns, or consumer profiling.
  4. No Creditworthiness Assessment: Health Connect data is NEVER used to evaluate creditworthiness, consumer credit eligibility, or insurance underwriting.
  5. No Human Access: No human is permitted to read or inspect your Health Connect data under any circumstances.

4. Artificial Intelligence & Telemetry Processing Disclosures

⚡ 100% ON-DEVICE AI COACHING Whenever supported by your Android hardware, OdinEye runs Gemini Nano locally via Android AICore directly on your phone’s Neural Processing Unit (NPU). Biometrics and prompts never leave your device.
  • Android AICore / Gemini Nano: Local speech-to-text transcription and clinical reasoning execute directly on-device without internet access.
  • Offline Sports Science Engine: Fallback deterministic sports science rules engine runs locally with zero network requests.
  • Optional Cloud API Fallback: If you explicitly choose to provide a personal Google Gemini API key on unsupported legacy hardware, prompts are sent directly to Google AI Studio via encrypted HTTPS under Google's Terms of Service. This is disabled by default.

5. Cryptographic Vault & Storage Security

OdinEye applies defense-in-depth cryptographic security to safeguard your data:

  • AES-256-CBC: Industry-standard symmetric cipher encrypting SQLite databases at rest.
  • PBKDF2 Key Derivation: 10,000 rounds with HMAC-SHA256 and cryptographically random salts.
  • Android Hardware Keystore: Master cryptographic keys are generated and protected by hardware security enclaves.
  • Linux Mode 0700: Database files are isolated exclusively to the private application sandbox, inaccessible to other apps.

6. In-App Data Deletion & User Control

In full compliance with Google Play’s Account and Data Deletion Policy, you maintain complete and permanent authority to wipe all stored data at any moment without contacting support:

  1. Open OdinEye and tap the Config (Settings) tab.
  2. Scroll to Category 04: Hardware Security & Vault Audit.
  3. Tap Wipe Vault & Delete Keys.

What happens immediately: All encrypted API keys, medication reminders, mood check-ins, sleep caches, and hardware Keystore entries are permanently wiped. Because we maintain no cloud databases, no residual copies remain anywhere.

7. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Legal Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), users are entitled to rights of access, erasure, restriction, and portability:

  • Right of Access & Portability: All personal data is immediately accessible to you in the OdinEye UI and local database.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can erase all data instantly using the in-app "Wipe Vault" tool or by uninstalling the application.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: OdinEye does not sell or share personal health information. Zero commercial data brokers have access to your metrics.

8. Children’s Privacy Protection

OdinEye is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If you believe a minor has installed the Application without guardian consent, please contact us or delete the application from the device.
