Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026 · Package: com.odineye.health

Local-First, Zero-Knowledge Privacy Architecture

OdinEye does not operate developer servers or cloud databases. All health telemetry, credentials, and medication schedules are encrypted with AES-256 and stored solely on your device inside hardware-backed storage.

1. Google Play Health Connect Limited Use Policy

OdinEye strictly complies with Google Play's Health Connect Developer Policies:

  • Read-Only Access: Health Connect data is accessed strictly in read-only mode to visualize recovery scores, sleep depth, and cardiovascular telemetry.
  • Zero Data Sale: Health Connect data is never sold, rented, or leased to third parties, data brokers, or advertising exchanges.
  • Zero Advertising: Health Connect data is never used or disclosed for advertising, retargeting, profiling, or marketing.
  • Zero Credit Lending: Health Connect data is never used to evaluate creditworthiness.
  • Prohibition of Human Access: No human is permitted to inspect or read your health data.

3. Device Permissions & System Notifications

OdinEye utilizes standard Android OS permissions to deliver its wellness capabilities:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Delivers user-scheduled medication dose reminders and wellness alerts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Guarantees medication reminders fire at precise scheduled times even when the device enters low-power Doze mode.
  • VIBRATE: Provides gentle haptic feedback during guided breathing cycles.

4. Unilateral In-App Data Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

In adherence to Google Play's Account & Data Deletion rules, you retain total unilateral control over your records:

  1. Open OdinEye.
  2. Navigate to Settings (Config) → Category 04: Hardware Security & Vault Audit.
  3. Tap Wipe Vault & Delete Keys.

This action permanently and irreversibly deletes all stored credentials, medication schedules, mindfulness histories, and local caches from your device hardware.
